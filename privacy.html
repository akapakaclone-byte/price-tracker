Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PriceTracker ("we", "us", or "our") handles information used by our Discord bot and website.

1. Information we collect

PriceTracker is designed to collect the information needed to provide price-history features. This may include price information posted in configured Discord channels, the time a price was recorded, and technical identifiers needed to operate the bot and website.

We aim to avoid collecting usernames, private messages, or other personal information unless it is necessary for a specific feature.

2. How we use information

We use collected information to record price changes, calculate statistics, display price charts, maintain the service, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot technical problems.

3. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by hosting, database, or other service providers that are necessary to operate PriceTracker.

4. Data retention

Price history may be retained while the service is operating so that historical charts and statistics can be provided. Technical data is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for operating and securing the service.

5. Your rights and deletion requests

If you believe PriceTracker has stored personal information about you and you want it reviewed or deleted, contact the service owner using the contact method provided on the website. We will review reasonable requests and delete information where we are required or able to do so.

6. Discord

PriceTracker interacts with Discord's services and is subject to Discord's applicable terms and policies. PriceTracker is not operated by or affiliated with Discord.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information used by the service. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service or its data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact the service owner through the contact method listed on the PriceTracker website.
